Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about purchasing digital goods on Olaslog

How fast is delivery after payment? ▾
Fulfillment is 100% automated and instant. As soon as you click checkout from your wallet, our system decrypts your assigned credentials and displays them on your screen in under 3 seconds.
How does wallet funding work? ▾
You can fund your wallet with any amount starting from ₦100 via your dedicated Virtual Bank Account. Simply transfer to your account number from any Nigerian bank app and your balance is updated automatically.
What is your replacement or refund policy? ▾
Digital accounts do not carry perpetual warranties. However, if a credential is fundamentally defective upon delivery, you can use the "Report an Issue" link in your Orders tab with an attached screenshot proof. Our admin team will inspect it and credit your wallet.
Can I view my past credentials again? ▾
Yes! All your purchased credentials remain securely stored in your Orders & History section and can be revealed at any time.
